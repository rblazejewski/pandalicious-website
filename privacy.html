Privacy notice

Last updated 30 September 2026

This notice covers the PanDalicious website and its waitlist. The PanDalicious app has its own privacy policy, which you'll see when you sign up in the app.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address and the date you signed up. That's all. The website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Why we use it

We use your email address to tell you when PanDalicious launches on Kickstarter, and to send occasional updates about the app. We rely on your consent, which you give by joining the waitlist.

Who we share it with

Your email address is stored with Supabase, our database provider, which processes it on our behalf. We don't sell it or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your address until PanDalicious has launched and the waitlist has served its purpose, or until you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing hello@pandalicious.co.uk. Every email we send will also include a way to unsubscribe. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
